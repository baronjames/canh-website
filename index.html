01 / THERMAL RESEARCHIndustry-linked capstone

Heat pipes for fuel injector de-icing

Exploring passive heat transfer to address ice crystal formation on LPG fuel injector tips.

Heat transferExperimentationCFD
Project contributions +

Worked in a six-person student team coordinating with Fujikura Ltd. The project combined a literature review and risk assessment with the design and proposal of a heat-transfer experiment.

Conducted physical experiments and CFD work to investigate the heat required, and analysed a potential engine-bay heat source for passive de-icing using heat pipes.

02 / MECHANICAL DESIGNAcademic team project

A practical, portable solar panel stand

Developing a stand around panel weight, solar angle and transportability, from early sketches to an assembled CAD design.

SolidWorksAssembliesTechnical drawings
Project contributions +

Collaborated with three teammates to combine design ideas into a SolidWorks assembly. The project included individual part drawings and tolerances, force analyses, an estimated bill of materials and final technical drawings for manufacturing.

Applying Australian Standards to a defined set of operating conditions, with supporting calculations and a detailed 3D model.
